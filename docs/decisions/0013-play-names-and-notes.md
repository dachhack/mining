# 0013 — Play interface: names for choices, a shorter list, and "not on the list" notes

Date: 2026-11-18. Status: accepted with issues #30 and #20. Amends 0009 (the text play interface); what 0009 says about the play log is unchanged.

## Context

The first playtest round (m1-r1, finding 10) lost five actions in four runs to menu numbers that changed meaning inside a chain, found the action list long where it carried no choice, did not find the top layer the help said was known, and could not tell why a move was missing. Since then decision 0011 (the lamp) added a second reason for a missing move: a cell it is judged by has not been seen.

## Decision

- **Every choice has a name as well as a number.** The name is made from the action alone, seen from where the avatar stands (`e`, `up`, `mine-down`, `mine-ne-down`, `ladder-here`, `craft-lamp-oil`, `take-n-coal`, `drop-all-stone`, `leave`, `end`), so it means the same at every step. `--do` and the prompt take either. A name that is not on the list at that moment is refused and stops a chain; it is never read as another action. One name works without being printed: `drop-all-<item>` while exactly one is carried means `drop-<item>`, since that drops them all. Numbers are as in 0009: the engine's order from 1, and `0` for "end shift".
- **The list is still exactly `legalActions` plus "end shift"**, one choice each. Only the printing changed: mine choices with the same block, yield, traces, weight note and AP cost share one line that lists each one's number and name. The block underfoot always has its own line, because that line says what the fall costs.
- **Map.** With no `--radius`, the map is the whole cube (and one cell round it) while the avatar stands on the surface, and 4 cells each way underground as before. The "Map:" line says whether the player has seen cells of the drawn layers that are off the map, and which `--radius` shows them all; it reads only the revealed flags.
- **Labels.** Each layer's column is as wide as its map or its labels, whichever is wider (issue #20).
- **"Not on the list" notes.** One line for each move a player might look for that the engine does not offer: a walk into an open cell (the drop is too long, or what is under the cell is unseen), a step up onto rock whose top is unseen (one line for all such directions), a climb up with no ladder above, a climb down with a drop too long or unseen. A plain wall gets no line, and nothing is said when the avatar has no AP to move. Where a ladder in the cell would make the move possible the note says so, except on the surface, where nothing can be placed.
- **The notes explain; they do not decide, and they add no knowledge.** They are worked out in `tools/play/missing.ts` from revealed cells only, in the same order the engine judges a drop, and stop at the first unseen cell with "you have not seen ...". A test changes every unseen cell (rock to open, open to rock, ladders and loose items added) and requires the whole view text to stay the same.

## Consequences

- The play log is untouched: it holds the engine's actions, not numbers or names (format 1). Logs from m1-r1 were written by engine 0.1.0 and stop replaying part-way on engine 0.2.0, on `main` as well as here; that comes from the rule changes of 0010 to 0012, not from this change.
- `missing.ts` repeats the engine's drop walk (`knownFallDistance`), which is not on the engine's public surface. If the move rules change, the notes must be changed with them. A note is only ever given for a direction with no legal move, so a stale note can be wrong about the reason but cannot offer or hide an action.
- The first view of a run is longer (34 map rows instead of 9). It is that long only while the avatar is on the surface.

## Amended after playtest round m1-r2 (issues #47 and #46)

Round m1-r2 (evaluation findings 5, 7, 8, 10, 16, 17) and the review of PR #45 found places where the screen was wrong or silent. The changes are in `tools/play` only; no rule, action name or log format changed.

- **Notes: three more, and one exception to "only for a direction with no move".**
  - _A pit._ No move of any kind, rock on all four sides, open space overhead and no ladder in the cell: one line says so, and that a ladder in the cell and one in the cell above let the miner climb up. The advice is left out if either cell could not take a ladder.
  - _A step up that is not there._ Where the cell beside the miner is open, the cell on top of it and the cell overhead are both seen and open, the move that way on the list is a walk that drops, and no move up of any kind is on the list: "No step up north: there is no rock there to step onto". (Without the last condition it printed mostly beside a hole in layer 0 with a step up to the surface on the list: 29 of 33 times on the logs of round m1-r2.) This is the one note about a direction that does have a move; it is about the step up, not the walk.
  - _Too little AP._ One line names the actions that are missing only because they cost more AP than is left, grouped by cost. It is the engine's own `legalActions` asked a second time for the same state with a full shift's AP, less the list as it stands; the tool reads no cell for it. With 0 AP the line is short ("everything that costs AP"). This replaces "nothing is said when the avatar has no AP to move": the move notes are still left out then, and the AP line gives the reason.
- **Ladder advice only where a ladder can go**: the cell must be inside the grid and hold no structure (not where a torch is).
- **Traced rock keeps its rock type on the map.** This replaces the play-tool line of 0012 ("drawn as `%` in place of the rock's own character"). Each kind of traced rock has its own character: `D` dirt, `%` stone, `h` hard rock (plain: `d`, `#`, `H`). Still one character per cell; the legend names each. Chosen over a second map or a list of coordinates because the player plans AP from the map, and over digits for the AP cost because two rocks may one day cost the same. The characters are in `tools/play/symbols.ts`.
- **Lamp-out banner.** It says what the lamp no longer shows and that blocks seen earlier can still be mined; it no longer says "No diagonal mining". On the surface it is only "(lamp is out)". It reads the oil gauge and the miner's layer, nothing else.
- **Fuel.** When ending the shift would leave fuel at 0, the preview adds that the next shift is the last unless fuel is crafted (the rule is 0005 item 21, unchanged).
- **Fall warning.** Unchanged in what it says when: "1 block" when the cell under the landing is seen, "1 block or more" when it is not. In round m1-r2 that cell was unseen in 12 of the 13 unheld mines underfoot (checked by replaying the logs), because a lamp in a one-cell shaft shows the floor and not what is under it. The text now names the unseen cell's layer, and the help says why.
- **Map on the surface.** With no `--radius`, layer 0 is drawn alone, with `@` on the block the miner stands on; the surface, open air everywhere, is not drawn (loose items lying on it are still listed). This halves the width of the first view. `--radius N` draws the surface beside layer 0 as before.
- **Radius hint.** Printed only when the player has seen cells below the top layer that are off the map, and its number is the radius that shows those. The top layer is known from the start, so it neither triggers the hint nor sets its number; when it alone is cut off, the "Map:" line says nothing about what is beyond.
- **Drop choices.** `drop-<item>` and `drop-all-<item>` share one line of the list, as mine choices do. `drop-all-<item>` is read as `drop-<item>` only while exactly one is carried and nothing else is read that way (`drop-all-all-stone` is refused).
- **Wording.** A pick-up of part of a pile says "4 of the 5 ... (only 4 fit your carry limit)"; a fallen pile is "A loose pile of 1 Stone fell ..."; traces first seen in the step that mined a traces-giving ore are said to mean that other ore is near, because the engine notes traces after the mined ore has gone from the cube, so they cannot come from it (marks noticed earlier can go stale; that is owner question 54, and a different thing); the help gives the lamp-oil rate from `src/data`.
- **The leak test covers each of these**: one state for every new note and wording is in the list of states whose unseen cells are all changed, and a second test checks that each state does print its note.

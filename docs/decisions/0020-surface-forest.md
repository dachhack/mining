# 0020 — The surface forest: trees to cut, paths, meadows, outcrops; trees regrow

Date: 2026-11-19. Status: accepted with issue #70. The owner asked for it directly; design doc section 3 (not marked [Decided]) is changed in the same PR. Numbers marked [Default] live in `src/data/surface.ts`.

## Context

Owner, 2026-11-19: "We need the surface to be mostly trees you can cut for timber then this exposes rock or dirt or other. Trees grow back if their spot isnt mined in a few turns." Asked by the coordinator: trees block walking, "but there are dirt paths and stone outcroppings and meadows"; the ground under a tree is "Hidden until cut"; regrowth takes "10 shifts". Issue #70 gives the coordinator's reading in eight items; this note records how it was built and the choices the issue left open.

## Decision

1. **Surface cells.** Each of the 32 × 32 surface cells (`z = -1`) is a tree, a dirt path, a meadow, a stone outcrop, or (once cut) a stump. They are kept in two new state arrays, `surface` (the kind) and `stumpAge` (a stump's regrowth count), one entry per column; `STATE_VERSION` is 5. Meadow is code 0, so a blank state (every hand-built test cube) is open ground everywhere, as the surface was before.
2. **A tree is not a block.** `blockAt` still answers "open air" on the surface, so water, gas and air paths (M2 work in progress) treat the forest as open air, as they treated the bare surface. A tree matters only where a rule says so: walking and stepping up (`actions/move.ts`), light, reveal and looking over an edge (`reveal.ts`), mining from the surface (`actions/mine.ts`) and cutting (`actions/cutTree.ts`). The code is in `src/engine/surface/` (state helpers, generation, regrowth) and `src/engine/actions/cutTree.ts`.
3. **Generation** (`surface/generation.ts`, drawn from `state.rng` right after the cube): forest everywhere; the start cell and its eight neighbours meadow; 4–6 round meadows of radius 2–4, at least 6 steps from the start and 2 cells in from the edges; a winding path from the start to the centre of each meadow and to a random cell on each of two different edges (each step goes nearer its end, except up to 6 random turns aside with chance 0.3, so every path ends); then each tree cell over stone becomes an outcrop with chance 0.3. On 300 seeds the surface is 66.6% trees on average.
4. **Coal in sight near the start** (owner's decision on PR #72: "Coal visible near start"). After the outcrops, generation looks at the open surface cells within 8 steps of the start (walking over open ground only; [Default] `coalInSightWithinSteps`). If none of them has coal under it, one of them (not the start cell, not an outcrop) is picked at random and its layer-0 block becomes coal, replacing plain rock (dirt or stone) where the block under it is solid. This reads only open ground, which the player sees, plus layer 1's solidity (as the path rule does; every layer-1 cell is solid today). On seeds 1–1,000 coal was added on 487.
5. **Hidden information.** Where the trees, meadows and paths go reads nothing of the cube. Outcrops are the one rule that looks at layer 0, and an outcrop reveals the stone it stands on (issue #70 accepts that a tree is then a little less likely to stand over stone). A path wears plain stone under it to dirt; ore under a path is left, and shows. The ground under a tree is not revealed by daylight, the lamp's light (a tree stops light), reach from the surface, or looking over an edge; it cannot be mined from the surface. The property test in `tests/engine/properties.test.ts` sets every unseen block under a tree to coal and to open space and checks that the action list, the forecast, the text view and the tap map's tiles do not change.
6. **Cutting.** `cutTree` (2 AP, gives 2 timber) is offered on the surface for a tree in one of the four cells beside the avatar. Timber that does not fit the carry limit is left on the stump, as a mining yield is left in the hole. The stump is open ground; the block under it is revealed by daylight at once.
7. **Regrowth: where in the end of the shift.** After the cube phase and its `settle`, before upkeep, if the avatar is alive. So the end-shift forecast (`ShiftForecast.regrowth`, a new field) can say where a tree will grow, and a tree that grows is there at the start of the next shift.
8. **Regrowth: the count.** For each stump in storage order: if its layer-0 block is not solid, the count goes back to 0; otherwise it goes up by one, to at most 10; at 10, if neither the avatar nor loose items are on the stump, a tree grows and the count is cleared, else it waits at 10. A tree cut in shift N therefore grows at the end of shift N + 9 (the shift of the cut counts as one of the ten). A surface cell never holds a structure (structures are placed only in the grid), so "a ladder or other structure on it" reduces to the ground being open, which the first rule covers.

## Readings (the smallest reading where the issue, the design doc and 0014 left room)

1. **Mining under a tree from underground is allowed.** Issue #70 item 4 says mining _down from the surface_ is not offered under a tree. From a hole beside it, the block under a tree is revealed by light from the side and mined like any other; the tree then stands over a hole, and the stump left by cutting it never regrows until the hole is filled. A tree over such a hole is a roof: the miner in it has no headroom, so no step up out of it is offered (review of PR #72).
2. **"Mined in that time" is read as "its layer-0 block is not solid".** A block that is mined out and later filled again (rubble, sealant) starts the count again from 0. The check is made once, at the end of each shift, so ground mined and filled again within one shift does not reset the count, where "has not been mined in that time" would; no player gains by it (review of PR #72).
3. **The timber-root of 0014 item 32 is unchanged**; trees are a second source of timber.
4. **The text map on the surface draws the surface again.** Issue #46 item 2 left it out because it was open air everywhere; now it shows the forest (`Y` tree, `,` stump, `:` path, `"` meadow, `^` outcrop), beside layer 0.

## Consequences

- **Starting fuel (0014 item 33).** Coal under trees is no longer seen for free. Without item 4 the coal policy (taught to dig look-holes and cut trees when no coal is in sight) got its fuel back with the margin on 954 of 1,000 seeds; with item 4 it does on all 1,000, with 24 fuel (unchanged), and 4 fuel would do on every seed.
- Golden runs re-recorded (the start state changed, and recorded mines under trees became illegal), and a fifth added (`wander-seed-181-forest.json`) that cuts four trees and sees one grow back.
- **Path wear and M2-2 (upper-band voids).** A path wears stone to dirt only where the layer-1 cell under it is solid. Every cell of a cube generated today is solid, so this reads nothing. Once M2-2 adds voids it would give one away: a path cell over plain stone would tell the player that the unseen cell under it is open. M2-2 must change it, either by not wearing the ground at all (a path over whatever is there, like a meadow) or by wearing it regardless and letting dirt over a void settle where the player sees it. The coal-in-sight step (item 4) reads layer 1 the same way and needs the same change.
- **Trees and the M2 hazards.** A tree is open air for water, gas and the connected-air path to the surface (agreed in the review of PR #72). But any M2 rule that lets something enter or rest in a surface cell (water spreading across the surface, rubble, items thrown) must also check `hasTree`: a tree cell holds nothing.
- **Generator draws.** The outcrop step draws one number for every tree cell before it looks at the ground, so the generator's stream does not depend on what lies under the trees. The coal-in-sight step draws a number only when no coal is in sight, which the player sees.
- Saves from state version 4 do not load. `ENGINE_VERSION` stays 0.3.0 (it covers the whole M2 batch).
- The paused M2 tasks: `ShiftForecast` has a new `regrowth` field; `actions/move.ts`, `actions/mine.ts`, `reveal.ts`, `state.ts`, `serialise.ts`, `index.ts`, `actionTypes.ts` and `events.ts` have small local additions. Path cells wear stone to dirt only where layer 1 is solid, so a cube with upper-band voids (M2-2) stays at rest.

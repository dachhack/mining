# 0042 — UI lab 2: fog of war around camps, camps you place and upgrade

Date: 2026-11-21. Status: implemented (issue #118). No game rule, game data number or golden hash changes; `ENGINE_VERSION` stays 0.4.0, and nothing under `src/engine` changed.

## What

- **A third button on the start screen**, "Lab 2" (`#lab2`, or `#lab2/110` for seed 110). Lab 1 and the game are unchanged.
- **Lab 2 is Lab 1 plus camps** (`src/app/lab/campWorld.ts`). Same cube, blocks, shadows, tap-to-mine, level rule and counter, drawn by the same code.
- **Fog.** Each camp lights a circle of squares. Lit squares are drawn as in Lab 1 and can be mined. Squares lit once but not now are drawn grey and cannot be mined. The rest is dark fog: taps there do nothing at all. The edge of the lit area has a thin warm line, a soft shade inside it and a little light spilling into the dark.
- **Camps.** A marker (a tent on a disc, with the level in a tag) on their square, which is never mined. A tap on a camp opens its panel: level, ring, what it mines, what the next level gives, the upgrade's cost (what you have of each item, out of what it costs), an "Upgrade" button and "Pack up". The camp tool ("New camp", or a long press on a lit square) asks for a lit square, then shows the new ring (dashed), the squares it would light, the cost, and a "Place camp" button.
- **Mining ability** of a square is the best level among the camps whose ring covers it. A block too hard for that level is refused with its own cue: a blue padlock, "Needs Lv 2" floating up, and a blue hint ("Hard rock needs a level 2 camp over it"). The too-steep refusal keeps Lab 1's yellow cue and shake.

## Choices

- **A circle, not face steps.** A diamond of face steps looks like a rotated square on a square grid; a circle reads as "the reach of a lantern". `inCircle` counts a square when dx² + dy² ≤ r² + r (a circle of about r + ½), which leaves no single squares poking out at the four points. Radius 3 lights 37 squares, 7 across.
- **Starting numbers** (all in `src/data/labCamps.ts`, which the game never reads):

  | Level | Ring | Adds                                                              | Cost to reach it                      |
  | ----- | ---- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------- |
  | 1     | 3    | dirt, rubble, stone, coal seam, timber-root                       | new camp: 8 stone (the first is free) |
  | 2     | 5    | hard rock                                                         | 12 stone, 2 timber                    |
  | 3     | 8    | iron ore, copper ore, crystal (and unstable ore, relic, keystone) | 30 stone, 6 timber, 3 coal            |

  Timber-root is at level 1 so timber can pay for upgrades. A new camp costs stone only: the first ring holds no timber in most seeds (owner question 132). A simple bot (mine everything, upgrade when it can, place each camp where it lights the most new squares; up to 15 camps) has its first level-2 camp by its 5th camp in 28 of 40 seeds, and a level-3 camp in 26 of 40, after 7 camps or so (1 to 14); coal is what runs short.

- **The ring is also a depth limit.** Squares outside every ring cannot be mined, so they stay level, and the level rule lets the ground fall only 1 per square from them: a ring of radius r can be dug about r levels deep in its middle, less near the camp's own square. Hard rock first appears at layer 4 (the middle band), so a level-1 camp alone never meets it; the "needs a level 2 camp" cue appears when several level-1 rings overlap, and "needs a level 3 camp" once a level-2 camp digs into the ore.
- **Pack up** is how a square becomes grey: the camp goes, gives nothing back, and cannot be the last one (owner question 133).
- **The map never moves** (as Lab 1's review asked). Everything under the map is one tray of a fixed height (164 px); the key, the camp tool, its confirm bar and a camp's panel only swap what is inside it, each row one line high. An e2e test checks the map's and the tray's boxes after each panel opens and closes, and after taps.
- **Shared code with Lab 1.** Lab 1's screen was split into `labDraw.ts` (a square, its shadows, the tap cues), `labGestures.ts` (drag, pinch, wheel, tap, and an optional hold) and `labDom.ts` (title bar, counter, key). Lab 1 uses them unchanged in behaviour; its e2e tests pass as before.
- **A prepared world for the e2e tests.** `{ seed, steps }` in localStorage under `mining-game.lab2.prepared` opens Lab 2 with those steps replayed (taps, placings, upgrades). The upgrade test uses it to start where a level-2 camp has dug into ore, rather than tapping 900 squares. "New world" removes it; players never have one.

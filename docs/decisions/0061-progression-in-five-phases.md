# 0061: Progression in five phases

Date: 2026-11-23. The owner, in a single conversation (no agent fleet), set the arc of a run. The plan, with every default filled in, is `docs/plans/progression-phases.md`.

## What the owner decided

- **Five phases:** camps and the landscape; farming and planning the pit; tier-1 contracts and tools; tier-2 contracts and a deep camp; tier-3 contracts and money.
- **Camps** stand only on the top three levels (depth 0 to 2). A base camp mines only dirt, clay and rubble: not stone or coal. A new deep camp type, later, may stand at any depth, and costs goods only the market sells.
- **A market** opens on the shift after night, every 10 shifts while the best camp is level 1, every 5 at level 2, every 3 at level 3. It trades for items the player cannot get from their own land. A "day" is a shift.
- **Tools are bought** at the market to mine harder rock and rarer ore. A smelter lets the player fill ingot contracts, not only ore ones, and make some of those items for less than they cost to buy.
- **Farming:** grass regrows, saplings are planted, and animals are kept in pens, fed with hay made from grass.
- **A run ends** after 100 shifts, or when the crew is lost.

## What it changes in the design doc

Four [Decided] items change, with the owner's approval, as each is built: the camp table (section 6), where camps may stand (section 2, Base), what the smelter is for (section 6), and how a run ends (sections 5.1 and 8: a contract pays coins and no longer ends the run). The iron drill's meaning changes from "one camp level higher" to "mines ore", so question 166's "keep the drill as it is" is superseded.

## Defaults filled in

The design choices the owner did not make are owner questions 167 to 176, status `assumed`. The main ones: one currency (coins), and the score is the coins held at the end; each trade costs 1 tap; ore needs an iron drill and the deepest blocks a steel drill, so camp levels gate only the upper band; one contract held at a time, with no deadline.

# Playtest: cautious, seed 23

- Interface: text
- Engine version / commit: engine 0.1.0 / 1add9f7
- Outcome: salvage (left the cube alive at shift 19 with 7 Coal, 6 Timber, 6 Ladder; no ore)
- Shifts played: 19
- Deepest layer reached: 8

## What I was trying to do

Same aim as on seed 11: layer 8, something worth having, out alive, no avoidable risk. Same method: a staircase I can walk back up, never mining the block I stand on.

This time the top layer showed a line of five coal seams four cells east of the start, so the plan opened with collecting those from the surface (mining "one layer down" while standing on top), then using the trench they left as the first stair.

The plan changed once. Layer 8 had no ore in sight, so I gave myself until the end of shift 15 to find some by tunnelling sideways, and went home when that found nothing.

## Key moments

- Shift 1: took two timber-roots from the surface, then walked east. Expected surface gathering to be slow; it cost a shift and a half in all.
- Shifts 2 to 4: mined five coal seams at x20 from the surface without going down. No risk at all, and the "if you end the shift now" line showed fuel barely moving.
- Shift 5: on layer 1 with coal to the north and coal to the south, and those two blocks were also the only two treads I could step up from. I mined the north one and checked I could still get out (through the cell it opened, then up into the trench) before mining the south one on shift 6.
- Shift 5 and shift 10: had 1 AP left over each time and spent it on "Craft Fuel (x3) from 1 Coal". Fuel went 20 to 23 and 18 to 21. This is the best use of a spare AP I found.
- Shift 9: two timber-roots on layer 5 (x24 y14 and y15), deeper than I expected from "only in the upper layers".
- Shift 12: reached layer 8. Everything in view on layers 8 and 9 was stone or hard rock.
- Shift 12: slip. I chained "5,1" meaning mine west, then move west. Choice 1 was still "Step up east to layer 7". Cost 2 AP and the rest of the shift.
- Shifts 13 to 15: tunnelled along layer 8 (south one cell, west two cells, 2 AP of stone each). Each new cell showed about six new blocks. All stone and hard rock. Hard rock costs 3 AP and gives 1 Stone.
- Shift 15: turned round as I had told myself to, with fuel 16 and six coal.
- Shifts 18 to 19: back on the surface with AP to spare and 8 weight free, walked three cells to take the last surface coal seam, then left.

## How it ended

Left from the surface on shift 19: health 10/10, fuel 12, lamp oil 2. I reached the depth I wanted but brought back only coal and timber. I could not tell whether layer 8 was empty here or whether I had been unlucky with the twenty or so cells I opened.

## Confusing

- Shift 12 to 15: no way to tell where ore might be. On seed 11 copper was simply in view when I arrived at layer 7; here nothing was, and there is no hint which direction is better. Prospecting was a blind 3 AP per cell.
- Shift 12: hard rock gives plain Stone. I expected the slower rock to give something better.
- Shift 18: standing on the surface at x21 y15, "Move west" was not on the list. It took me a moment to see why: the cell to the west is over my own trench and stair, the drop there is 3, and long drops are never offered. Nothing on screen says so; the move is just missing.
- Shift 1 onward: as on seed 11, ending a shift with unspent AP gives fuel back, so I never understood fuel as a clock.

## Tedious or automatic

- Shifts 13 to 15, prospecting: mine stone, move, look, nothing, repeat. Nothing to decide except when to give up.
- Shifts 15 to 18, the climb out: fifteen moves, one sensible choice each time.
- Dropping stone every second action in the tunnel.
- Entering one choice per command after the slip on shift 12.

## Interesting

- Shift 5: working out that mining both coal blocks beside me would remove both ways up, and finding the third way before taking the second coal.
- Shift 12: whether to prospect or leave at once. A stricter reading of "leave when in doubt" would have left on shift 12. I stayed because a level tunnel has no fall in it and fuel was far from short.
- A better play I did not take: go on down. With fuel 16 and coal for 18 more, layers 9 to 12 were well within reach and probably held ore. I had no information about what is below and my target was 8, so I stopped.
- Another I did not take: on shift 11 I could have placed a ladder and mined straight down from layer 6 to save the stone on the stairs. I kept to stairs.

Limits, plainly:

- Fuel: never limited anything. Lowest was 12, on the surface, after crafting two coal into fuel that I did not strictly need.
- Lamp oil: fell to 2 by shift 16. I noticed it and would have crafted oil from coal if the trip had gone on; it changed nothing I did.
- Carry weight: 16/20 at most, and only because of stone. Not a limit.
- Ladders: none used.
- Timber: none used; I collected four more than I started with out of habit.

## Possible bugs

- None seen beyond the fuel refund noted above and in the seed 11 report (a shift with all AP unspent costs no fuel, and with the banked counter at 1 or 2 it is shown as a gain).

# Playtest: cautious, seed 37

- Interface: text
- Engine version / commit: engine 0.1.0 / 1add9f7
- Outcome: salvage (left the cube alive at shift 15 with 7 Coal, 4 Raw iron ore, 2 Timber, 6 Ladder)
- Shifts played: 15
- Deepest layer reached: 8

## What I was trying to do

Same aim and same method as seeds 11 and 23: a walkable staircase to layer 8, pick up what I can see on the way, leave alive.

The top layer showed nothing near the start, so I went straight down. The plan did not change; by this third run the staircase was routine.

## Key moments

- Shifts 1 to 2: three layers in two shifts through dirt.
- Shift 3: a coal seam one layer down to the west, directly under the tread I would need to step up on later. On seed 11 I left blocks like this alone. The help text names only falls and fuel as ways to die, and two runs had shown no rock moving, so this time I took it. On shift 14 I stepped up over that tread and it had held.
- Shift 4: reaching layer 4 showed four coal seams on layer 5.
- Shifts 5 to 8: mined six more coal, four of them from the layer above with "one layer down". Used a spare AP on shift 5 to craft fuel (20 to 23).
- Shift 9: on layer 7, an "i" appeared on layer 8, north-east and one down. The legend named it Iron ore.
- Shift 10: took that iron from layer 7 for 2 AP. Ended the shift with 1 AP unspent and the banked counter at 2: the end-of-shift line read "Fuel +1 (banked AP)". First time the banking did something I could see.
- Shift 11: stepped down into layer 8 and found two more iron beside the first.
- Shift 12: a fourth iron showed one cell further north; took it. Pack 17/20.
- Shift 13: walked one cell east to look past the pocket (1 AP, no risk). Stone only. Turned round.
- Shifts 13 to 15: twelve moves up and out. Left with fuel 14.

## How it ended

Left from the surface on shift 15: health 10/10, fuel 14, lamp oil 3. Nothing threatened the run at any point.

## Confusing

- Shift 3: still no way to know whether mining under a block is safe except by trying it.
- Shift 14: on the way up I passed a coal seam at x17 y15 on layer 4 that I had seen from below on shift 7. I could not reach it: the only cell beside it that I could stand in was the open cell above my own stair hole, where I would drop back down. Not wrong, but I only saw the problem when I was standing next to it.
- Lamp oil: 6 to 3 again, with no effect I could notice.

## Tedious or automatic

- Shifts 13 to 15: the climb out, one choice per move.
- Layers 6 and 7 (shifts 8 to 9): stone on every side, the same three actions per layer.
- Dropping stone, five times.
- Overall this run felt like repeating seed 11. Once the staircase method is known, the descent has one sensible move at almost every step; the only choices were which seams were worth a detour.

## Interesting

- Shift 3: the coal under my own tread, described above. It was the one place I knowingly took a risk I could have avoided, and I would count it against my persona.
- Shift 13: whether to prospect further. I had four ore, three weight free, and seed 23 had taught me that blind tunnelling finds little, so one look and then home.
- A better play I did not take: stay and go deeper. Fuel 16 plus seven coal is twenty or more further shifts, and the pack had room if I had crafted coal into fuel as I went. I left because the aim was met.

Limits, plainly:

- Fuel: never limited anything. Lowest was 14.
- Lamp oil: never limited anything.
- Carry weight: reached 17/20; would have become the limit with two more ore.
- Ladders: none used.
- Timber: none used, none collected.

## Possible bugs

- None new. The fuel refund for unspent AP is as described in the seed 11 report: a shift with all 5 AP unspent is shown as costing no fuel, or as gaining 1 when the banked counter is at 1 or 2 (seen in the end-of-shift line at shifts 8 and 10).

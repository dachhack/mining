# Playtest: Greedy, seed 23

- Interface: text
- Engine version / commit: engine 0.1.0 / 1add9f7
- Outcome: salvage (left alive with 4 Crystal and 8 Raw iron ore, weight 20 of 20, fuel 6)
- Shifts played: 45
- Deepest layer reached: 26

## What I was trying to do

Same aim as seed 11: the deep band, and out with the most valuable load. Seed 11 had ended with fuel 0 and nothing from the deep band, so this time fuel had hurt me and I allowed myself one precaution: I printed the whole top layer (`--radius 16`) before moving, to look for coal and timber.

The top layer showed a column of Coal seam at x20 y12–16, four cells east of the start, and five Timber-root in a clump at x14–16 y14–15, two cells north. Plan: timber, then coal, then a shaft straight down from the coal.

How it changed: it mostly did not. I found crystal next to the shaft at layer 23 and iron and copper veins higher up, and ended with a full pack. The limit this time was carry weight, not fuel.

## Key moments

- Shift 1–2. Stood on x15 y14 and mined all five Timber-root without moving again. Crafted ladders with spare AP over the next shifts (27 ladders in the end).
- Shift 4–7. Mined 7 Coal: five from layer 0 and two from layer 1 under the seam. Coal weighs 1. Six became fuel (+18) and one became lamp oil. Began the shaft at x20 y15 on shift 7 with fuel 30. Expected fuel to stop being a worry; it did.
- Shift 8. From layer 1, "Place a ladder in the cell above you" put a ladder in layer 0, where a step-up would have done. One ladder wasted; no harm.
- Shift 13. Layer 9: three Copper ore along the south side of the shaft and one more at layer 8. I left them for the way back because copper weighs 2 and I wanted the room for deep-band finds.
- Shift 14–18. Layers 14–16: Iron ore on the north and west sides of the shaft on all three layers; the shaft itself went through one (1 Raw iron ore, weight 2). Left the rest for the way back.
- Shift 24–25. At layer 22 the "below" pane showed a Crystal at x19 y15, layer 23. I was by then repeating the same four choices every shift and I kept going down past it, to layer 26 on shift 29, hoping for more or better underneath. Layers 24–27 showed only Hard rock and Stone.
- Shift 29–30. Used my last ladder in layer 26, climbed back to 23 and mined the Crystal from the ladder (3 AP). Crystal weighs 1, half of an ore. Mining it showed another Crystal behind it.
- Shift 31–33. Followed the vein west and north-west: four Crystal in all, each revealed by taking the one before. After the fourth, every neighbouring cell was Hard rock.
- Shift 34–35. Dug two more blocks west on layer 23 looking for the vein to pick up again. Nothing. Fuel 14; I decided to stop prospecting and fill the pack with what I knew was there.
- Shift 39–42. From the ladder at layer 15, mined seven Iron ore without leaving the shaft (four on my layer, three one layer down). Pack 20/20. The action list then marked further blocks "too heavy to carry: it will be left lying there". The copper at layer 9 stayed in the rock; I had no room.
- Shift 45. Stepped out and left with fuel 6.

## How it ended

Left alive on shift 45: 4 Crystal, 8 Raw iron ore, fuel 6, health 10/10. Nothing threatened me at any point.

## Confusing

- I still do not know what anything is worth. I filled 16 of 20 weight with iron because it was nearest. Whether 8 iron beats, say, 4 iron and 4 copper, or whether I should have spent the 6 spare fuel hunting a fifth crystal and thrown iron away for it, I cannot tell from the game.
- Shift 25: the crystal first appeared in the "below" pane, one layer down and one cell to the side. It is one letter in a 3×3 block of H and # and I had stopped reading the map closely. Nothing else on the screen says "new material seen".
- Shift 30–33: a vein is only visible one block at a time. I could not tell after the fourth crystal whether the vein had ended or turned a corner out of sight.
- Shift 32: lamp oil hit 0 and the line says "you see only one step around you". In a tunnel one block wide I could not notice any difference.

## Tedious or automatic

- Shifts 8–29: 22 shifts of "Mine below you / Place a ladder in the cell above you / Drop Stone / End". There was only one sensible move, and I chained the same choices several shifts at a time. This is how I went three layers past the crystal.
- Shifts 36–38 and 42–45: only "Move" and "Climb up".
- Crafting ladders three at a time, one AP per timber, five times.

## Interesting

- Shift 13 and 14–18: leaving copper and iron in the wall on the way down, betting on something lighter and better below. It paid.
- Shift 30: the first crystal weighing 1. That changed what I wanted the pack for.
- Shift 35: fuel 14, pack nearly empty, vein gone. Keep tunnelling blind in Hard rock at 4 AP a block, or go up and take the certain iron? I took two blind blocks and then the iron.
- Shift 42: full pack with copper still in reach higher up. The only way to take it was to drop iron, and I had no reason to prefer one to the other.

## Possible bugs

- None seen. Falls, ladders, step-ups, weights and the fuel preview all behaved as the help text says.

## Limits (asked for by the coordinator)

- Fuel: not a limit after the coal. Lowest was 6, at the end. It did set how long I was willing to prospect at shift 35.
- Lamp oil: ran out at the end of shift 32 even after one refill on shift 18. No effect I could see.
- Carry weight: the limit of this run. Full at shift 42; I left a copper vein and more iron behind. Stone had to be dropped every shift or two to keep room.
- Ladders: 27 made, the last used at layer 26 on shift 29. One wasted in layer 0. Had I wanted to go below 26 I would have had to cut steps.
- Timber: five from one spot was enough. There was more (three at x15–17 y11, two at layer 1, one at layer 4) that I did not need.
- Only one sensible move: yes, shifts 8–29 and both climbs.

## Better plays I saw and did not take

- Stop at layer 23 on shift 25 and mine the crystal at once; I lost about five shifts going down to 26 and back.
- Place the ladder in my own cell before mining under myself (26 blocks mined with the drop unseen).
- With fuel 6 left at the top and a known copper vein, a patient player could have gone back down for a second load. With a full pack and no way to store anything on the surface, I saw no point.

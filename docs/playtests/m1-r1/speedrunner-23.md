# Playtest: speed-runner, seed 23

- Interface: text
- Engine version / commit: 0.1.0 / 1add9f7
- Outcome: salvage (left alive at the surface; course completed: stood on layer 15, carried out 1 raw iron ore)
- Shifts played: 16 (15 was there for the taking; see "How it ended")
- Deepest layer reached: 15

Course for this round (no contract in this build): stand on layer 12 or deeper, pick up at least one iron or copper ore, return to the surface and leave, in as few shifts as possible. Second run; target was to beat 33 shifts from seed 11.

## What I was trying to do

Same straight shaft as seed 11, with what I learned there:

1. Look at the whole top layer first (it is all visible from the start) and take timber-root from the surface before digging, so ladders cannot run short.
2. Open a side cell on layer 0 so the last step out of the shaft needs no ladder.
3. Never place a ladder in my own cell on the way down; only in the cell above, with leftover AP.
4. If no ore is beside the shaft at layer 12, keep going straight down rather than tunnelling sideways. Each layer of shaft shows nine new cells and I now had ladders to spare.

The plan did not change during the run.

## Key moments

- **Shift 1.** The top-layer map showed timber-roots at x15 y15 and x16 y15, touching the start cell diagonally and to the north. From the surface I mined both ("north, one layer down" and "north-west, one layer down", 1 AP each) and got 2 timber without moving. The two holes also gave me the open side cell on layer 0 that I wanted. Then mined under my feet (stone, 2 AP) and crafted ladders with the last AP. Expected the surface mining to be awkward; it was the easiest two AP of the run.
- **Shifts 2 to 6.** Straight down, two blocks a shift through stone, ladder in the cell above with each spare AP.
- **Shift 5.** "Mine below you" started saying "(too heavy to carry: it will be left lying there)". The pack was at 19/20 with stone. No effect on digging.
- **Shift 7.** Layer 12 with 3 AP left. No ore in the 3×3 around the shaft on any layer from 8 to 13. Dropped 8 stone (free) so that ore would fit when I found it. Chose to go deeper rather than sideways. Expected two or three more layers of nothing.
- **Shifts 7 to 9.** Layers 13, 14, 15: all hard rock, 3 AP each.
- **Shift 9.** Landing on layer 15 showed five iron ore cells in the nine below me (layer 16, including the one under my feet). I mined the one to the north, one layer down (2 AP), from where I stood. The ore went straight into the pack; I never had to stand on layer 16 or ladder that layer.
- **Shifts 10 to 15.** Climbed: ladder above, climb, repeat. Needed 11 more ladders for layers 1 to 15 (four were already in from the descent); had 8 plus 2 timber, so one craft covered it.
- **Shift 15.** Reached layer 1 with 4 AP left, which was enough to step up into the timber hole on layer 0, step up to the surface, and leave. I did not do that; see below.

## How it ended

Left alive on shift 16 with 1 raw iron ore, 3 stone, 2 ladders, fuel 9, health 10/10.

It should have been shift 15. At layer 1 on shift 15 I was driving the climb with a repeated "place ladder above, climb" routine and carried it one step too far: I crafted three ladders I did not need, put one in the layer-0 cell and climbed it (3 AP), then ended the shift with 1 AP unspent while standing on layer 0, where "Step up to the surface" and "Leave" were both on the list. Stepping out on shift 16 cost one more shift. The play log shows it. That was my carelessness; the game offered the right actions the whole time.

Counting it up: the route as planned was 73 AP of actions, which fits in 15 shifts with 2 AP to spare.

## Limits: fuel, lamp oil, carry weight, ladders, timber

- **Fuel:** never a limit. Left with 9 of 24. There was a seam of coal four cells east of the start on the top layer; I did not bother with it.
- **Lamp oil:** never a limit. 3 of 6 left.
- **Carry weight:** the pack filled with stone by layer 10 (shift 5). I dropped all of it for free at layer 12. If I had not, the ore would have been left lying in its hole.
- **Ladders and timber:** not a limit this time, because of the two roots beside the start. With only the starting kit (12 ladders once crafted) I could not have laddered a shaft to layer 15; I would have had to stop the shaft at 12 and tunnel for ore instead.

## Confusing

- **Shift 9:** I was not sure whether ore mined from the layer above would drop into the hole or come to me. It came to me. Good for me, but I only knew after trying.
- Nothing else. After seed 11 the rules I needed were clear.

## Tedious or automatic

- **Shifts 2 to 9 (descent): one sensible move.** "Mine below you", and "ladder in the cell above" with the odd AP. The only decision in the whole descent was at layer 12: down or sideways.
- **Shifts 10 to 15 (ascent): one sensible move.** "Ladder above", "climb", fifteen layers. It was automatic enough that I stopped reading the screen, which is how I lost a shift at the top.

## Interesting

- Shift 1: reading the top-layer map to pick up timber before digging. It felt like the one place where looking carefully paid off.
- Shift 7: deeper or sideways. Deeper costs a ladder and 3 AP of hard rock per layer and shows nine cells; sideways costs about 4 AP per six cells and no ladder. Having spare ladders is what made "deeper" possible, and it found the ore in three layers.
- Shift 9: seeing that I could take the ore from above and skip a layer of digging, laddering and climbing.

## Shortcuts, dominant strategies, things that look exploitable

- **Timber from the surface.** All timber-roots on the top layer are visible at the start and the pick reaches layer 0 from the surface. Two AP gave me six ladders' worth of timber before I had gone anywhere. The hole a root leaves beside the shaft also saves a ladder at the top.
- **Mine ore from the layer above.** The pick reaches the nine cells below, and what it mines goes into the pack. I completed "pick up ore" without entering the ore layer.
- **Straight shaft with free falls** is still the only way down worth using: thirteen-plus layers for the price of the mining alone.
- **Free dropping:** stone is weight I can shed at no cost at any moment, so the carry limit only ever asks me to remember to press "drop" before mining something I want.
- **Fuel does not matter on this course.** 16 shifts used 15 fuel out of 24, and from seed 11 I know that 3 unspent AP pays for a shift anyway.
- **The run length was decided by where the ore was, not by anything I did.** Here it happened to be straight under the start at layer 16. On seed 11 it was nowhere within 220 cells.

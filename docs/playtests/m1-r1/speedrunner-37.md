# Playtest: speed-runner, seed 37

- Interface: text
- Engine version / commit: 0.1.0 / 1add9f7
- Outcome: salvage (left alive at the surface; course completed: stood on layer 12, carried out 1 raw copper ore)
- Shifts played: 11
- Deepest layer reached: 12

Course for this round (no contract in this build): stand on layer 12 or deeper, pick up at least one iron or copper ore, return to the surface and leave, in as few shifts as possible. Third run; target was to beat 16 shifts from seed 23.

## What I was trying to do

The same route as seed 23, trimmed to the minimum:

1. Check the top layer for timber beside the start. There was none within reach (nearest roots were four or five cells north), so I decided to go without: the starting kit makes exactly 12 ladders, which is exactly enough for layers 1 to 12 if the layer-0 cell needs none.
2. Spend 1 AP from the surface opening a side cell on layer 0, so I can step out of the top of the shaft without a ladder.
3. Straight down to layer 12, ladders in the cell above with spare AP.
4. Take ore from wherever it shows, from the layer above if possible. If none by layer 12, tunnel and pit at layer 12 (no spare ladders for a deeper shaft).

The plan did not change; ore showed up on arrival at layer 12.

## Key moments

- **Shift 1.** From the surface: "Mine east, one layer down" (dirt, 1 AP) to make the side cell on layer 0, then under my feet three times (dirt, dirt, stone). At layer 2 after one shift, same as the other seeds.
- **Shift 2.** The block under me at layer 4 turned out to be a coal seam (2 AP, got 1 coal). I expected to want it; I never used it. Fuel was never close to mattering.
- **Shifts 3 to 5.** Two blocks a shift, ladders into layers 6 and 8 with spare AP. One hard rock block (3 AP) at layer 10.
- **Shift 6.** Landed on layer 12 with 3 AP. The view showed copper ore in layer 13, diagonally below me to the south-east. The action read "gives 1 Raw copper ore (too heavy to carry: it will be left lying there)": pack was 19/20 with stone. Dropped 8 stone (free), the warning went away, mined the copper from where I stood (2 AP), and put a ladder in my own cell with the last AP. I expected to need a search here, as on seed 11; I needed none.
- **Shift 6, ladder count.** Ladders in at layers 6, 8 and 12; nine more cells to ladder (layers 1 to 5, 7, 9, 10, 11); I held 3 ladders and 2 timber, which is 9 ladders: exactly the number needed, with none over. Had the ore been one layer further down and out of reach, I would have had to choose between a tunnel and going back for timber.
- **Shifts 7 to 11.** Climb: "ladder above", "climb", with two crafts on the way.
- **Shift 11.** At layer 1: "Step up east to layer 0" into the side cell from shift 1, "Step up east to the surface", "Leave". 3 AP unspent.

## How it ended

Left alive on shift 11 with 1 raw copper ore and 1 coal, fuel 14, lamp oil 4, health 10/10, no ladders or timber left.

The run used 52 AP of actions. Ten full shifts are 50 AP, so 11 shifts is the fewest this route allows on this seed; I do not see 2 AP I could have saved.

## Limits: fuel, lamp oil, carry weight, ladders, timber

- **Fuel:** no. 14 of 24 left.
- **Lamp oil:** no. 4 of 6 left.
- **Carry weight:** it would have cost me the ore at shift 6 if I had not read the warning on the action. Dropping the stone was free, so in practice it limited nothing.
- **Ladders and timber:** exactly enough, nothing spare. This was a deliberate gamble to save the 4 to 6 AP a trip to the nearest roots would have cost. It limited what I could have done if the ore had not been there: no deeper shaft.

## Confusing

- Nothing in this run. By the third seed every action did what I expected.

## Tedious or automatic

- **The whole run was one sensible move at a time.** Shift 1 had a choice (where to put the side cell, whether to fetch timber). After that: "mine below you" thirteen times, one "mine the ore", then "ladder above" and "climb" eleven times. I made the route decisions before the first action and never had a reason to revisit them.
- Shift 2's coal and the top layer's timber were things I could have gone for and had no reason to.

## Interesting

- Shift 1: whether to walk north for timber or trust the kit. Twelve ladders for twelve cells with no margin was a real bet, and it is the only thing in this run I would call a decision.
- Shift 6: the "too heavy to carry" note on the ore action. A small thing, but it was the one moment where reading the list carefully changed the outcome.

## Shortcuts, dominant strategies, things that look exploitable

Everything that worked on seed 23 worked again, and I think this is now simply the route:

- **Side cell on layer 0 from the surface (1 AP)** replaces the top ladder, which makes the starting kit exactly sufficient for a layer-12 shaft.
- **Straight shaft, free one-block falls:** the descent to layer 12 cost 24 AP of mining and nothing else, done early on shift 6 on all three seeds (shift 6, 7, 6).
- **Ladders in the cell above with spare AP** on the way down, so no AP is lost at shift ends.
- **Ore mined from the layer above** goes straight into the pack.
- **Drop stone for free** whenever the pack matters.
- **Fuel, lamp oil, coal and health played no part.** I ignored all four and nothing happened.
- The difference between 33, 16 and 11 shifts across my three runs was almost entirely where the ore was (and one ladder miscount on seed 11). The route itself did not vary and I do not think it could be much faster: layer 12 and back with ore is about 52 AP when the ore is beside the shaft.
- **Looked exploitable, not needed here:** ending shifts with 3 AP unspent to play on with no fuel (used on seed 11); taking timber from the surface without going underground (used on seed 23).
